Privacy Policy

Neon Survivors • effective October 19, 2026 • Português
In short: we store your account and progress so the game works on any device; we verify purchases so nobody creates premium currency for free; ads appear only when you ask for a reward and are never personalized. We do not ask for your name, email, phone, location, contacts or photos. You can delete your account at any time, inside the game.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Remember Games, the developer of Neon Survivors on Google Play. For any privacy matter, including your rights under Brazil's LGPD (Law 13,709/2018), email gabrielinnocencio22@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Account identifier — a random ID created on first launch (anonymous account); if you create a recovery code, also a username and password derived from it. Used to open your account and let you return to it on another device.
  • Game progress — your profile (items, currencies, missions, game settings) and match logs (your inputs in each mission). Used to save the game and to verify results on the server (anti-cheat).
  • Social — the name you choose, your friend code, friends list, messages with friends and in clans, your Market listings. Seen by the other players involved.
  • Purchases — the Google Play receipt (order number, product, date and purchase token). Used to deliver what you bought and to stop a receipt from being used twice. We never receive card data.
  • Technical data — IP address, device model and OS, received when connecting. Used for connectivity, co-op play and security.

We do not ask for or store your real name, email, phone, precise location, contacts, photos, audio or payment card data.

Third-party components in the game process some technical data on their own to work: the purchases SDK (Unity IAP) uses approximate region (from IP), device identifiers, purchase history and crash/performance logs; the ads SDK (Unity LevelPlay) uses approximate region, the advertising ID and ad interactions (see section 3).

3. Ads

Neon Survivors only shows rewarded ads, and only when you tap to watch one. There are no ads that show up on their own and no banners. Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and its partner networks, which receive device data such as the Android advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate region it indicates), model, OS and your interactions with the ad, to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. Our ads are never personalized: the game tells the ad network there is no consent for personalization and that data must not be sold. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

4. Who we share data with

  • Unity Technologies — game servers (account, cloud save, game server and co-op connections — Unity Gaming Services) and ads (Unity LevelPlay). Unity's policy.
  • Google — purchases and app distribution (Google Play). Google's policy.
  • LevelPlay partner ad networks, only when you watch an ad, as described above.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell your data. Providers' servers may be outside Brazil; transfers follow LGPD article 33 with the providers' contractual safeguards.

5. Retention

While your account exists. When you delete it, we delete your profile, progress, social data and Market listings. Only purchase records (order number, product, date and receiving account) are kept, for up to 5 years as required by tax law and to prevent fraud.

6. Children and teenagers

Neon Survivors is meant for ages 13 and up. We never target ads based on profiles, we sell no paid loot boxes and all ads are optional. If you are the guardian of a child under 13 who created an account, email us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You may request confirmation of and access to your data, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and review of automated decisions. Deletion is available in the game (Profile → Options → Account). For other requests, email us with your friend code (Profile → Friends). We answer within 15 days. You may also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD).

8. Security

Connections are encrypted. Official progress lives on the server, where the device cannot change it. Your recovery code is stored only on your device: anyone with the code can enter your account, so keep it private.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for important changes, we will tell you in the game.
