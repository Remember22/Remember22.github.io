Privacy Policy

Neon Survivors • effective October 20, 2026 • Português
In short: Neon Survivors is for adults 18 and over only. We store your account and progress so the game works on any device; we verify purchases so nobody creates premium currency for free; ads appear only when you ask for a reward and are personalized only if you allow it — the game asks on first launch (in Europe, through Google's consent platform), and you can change it at any time in Profile → Options → Privacy. We do not ask for your name, email, phone, location, contacts or photos. You can delete your account at any time, inside the game.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Remember Games, the developer of Neon Survivors on Google Play. For any privacy matter, including your rights under Brazil's LGPD (Law 13,709/2018), email gabrielinnocencio22@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Account identifier — a random ID created on first launch (anonymous account); if you create a recovery code, also a username and password derived from it. Used to open your account and let you return to it on another device.
  • Age and consent — the confirmation that you are 18 or older and how it was made (by the birth date you type, which is not stored, or by Google Play's age signal — only the result and its date) and your choice about personalized ads (yes or no, its date and the version of the question's text). Kept on the device and in your account, so the game stays adults-only and we can demonstrate your consent, as the LGPD requires (legal obligation, art. 7 II and art. 8 §2).
  • Game progress — your profile (items, currencies, missions, game settings) and match logs (your inputs in each mission). Used to save the game and to verify results on the server (anti-cheat).
  • Social — the name you choose, your friend code, friends list, messages with friends and in clans, your Market listings. Seen by the other players involved.
  • Purchases — the Google Play receipt (order number, product, date and purchase token). Used to deliver what you bought and to stop a receipt from being used twice. We never receive card data.
  • Technical data — IP address, device model and OS, received when connecting. Used for connectivity, co-op play and security.

We do not ask for or store your real name, email, phone, precise location, contacts, photos, audio or payment card data.

Third-party components in the game process some technical data on their own to work: the purchases SDK (Unity IAP) uses approximate region (from IP), device identifiers, purchase history and crash/performance logs; the ads SDK (Unity LevelPlay) uses approximate region, the advertising ID and ad interactions — and, only with your consent, uses that data to personalize ads (see section 3).

3. Ads

Neon Survivors only shows rewarded ads, and only when you tap to watch one. There are no ads that show up on their own and no banners.

Who serves the ads: Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies' ad mediation) and the ad networks connected to it in the game, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads, also by Unity Technologies. To show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud, they receive device data such as the Android advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate region it indicates), model, OS and your interactions with the ad. If another ad network is added to the game, this list changes first.

Personalized ads, only with your consent. On first launch, after the age check, the game asks whether the ad network may use this data to show ads based on your interests. “Not now” and “Allow” have the same size and weight, and the reward is the same either way.

  • With “Allow”, the game tells the networks there is consent: they may use the advertising ID and ad interactions to choose the ads you see and, under their own policies, combine them with data from other apps and sites.
  • With “Not now” (or while you have not answered), the game tells the networks there is no consent and that data must not be sold or shared for advertising: ads are generic, not based on your profile.

In Europe (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform, a platform certified under the IAB TCF standard) appears instead of the game's question. There you choose, by purpose and by partner, what the ad networks may do; your choice is stored on the device, and the networks read it. Google uses your IP address (approximate region) to know whether you are in Europe. To change it: Profile → Options → Privacy → Personalized ads → MUDAR (Change), which opens the same form.

To withdraw consent (or give it later): Profile → Options → Privacy → Personalized ads. The change applies to the next ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads): without it, no ad is personalized. Unity's policy, covering LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Who we share data with

  • Unity Technologies — game servers (account, cloud save, game server and co-op connections — Unity Gaming Services) and ads (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads). Unity's policy.
  • Google — purchases and app distribution (Google Play) and, in Europe, the ads consent platform (User Messaging Platform), which receives your IP address and your choice. Google's policy.
  • The ad networks in section 3, only when you watch an ad, as described there.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell your data. With your consent to personalized ads, the ad networks use device data to personalize ads — what some laws (such as California's) call “sharing for advertising”; without it, the game tells the networks that data must not be sold or shared. Providers' servers may be outside Brazil; transfers follow LGPD article 33 with the providers' contractual safeguards.

5. Retention

While your account exists. When you delete it, we delete your profile, progress, social data, Market listings and the age and consent record kept in the account. Only purchase records (order number, product, date and receiving account) are kept, for up to 5 years as required by tax law and to prevent fraud. On the device, the age confirmation, the result of the store's age signal and the ads choice stay until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

6. Adults 18 and over only

Neon Survivors is for adults 18 and over only. On first launch, before anything else, the game asks for your birth date (the year; the month and day only when the year is not enough). Anyone who enters an age under 18 cannot play: no account is created, nothing is sent to the store or to the ad networks, and the answer is kept on the device. The birth date is not stored, only the result.

Google Play's age signal. On Android, each time the game opens, it asks Google Play (the Play Age Signals API) for the age range of your Google account — Google only shares it if you (or the parent, on a supervised account) allow it, and may ask in its own window. We use this signal only for the age rule: if the store says under 18, the game does not open (the notice stays, and comes back on every launch while the store says under 18); if the store says 18 or older, verified by Google, the game does not ask for your birth date; without the signal (the account does not share it, no connection or an error), the birth-date question applies. We keep only the result: on the device, “the store said under 18” or “18 or older, verified” and the date; in your account, only that adulthood was confirmed by the store and when. The age range never leaves the device, is never sent to ad networks or analytics and is not used to personalize anything (as Google's terms require).

The game is not made for children or teenagers, has no paid loot boxes, and all ads are optional. If you know of an account belonging to someone under 18, email us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You may request confirmation of and access to your data, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information about sharing, information about the option not to consent (for ads, the reward does not change), withdrawal of consent, and review of automated decisions. Ads consent can be changed in the game (Profile → Options → Privacy), and so can deletion (Profile → Options → Account). For other requests, email us with your friend code (Profile → Friends). We answer within 15 days. You may also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD). If you are in Europe, you also have your GDPR rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawing consent at any time — and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted. Official progress lives on the server, where the device cannot change it. Your recovery code is stored only on your device: anyone with the code can enter your account, so keep it private.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for important changes, we will tell you in the game. The October 20, 2026 version made the game adults-only, added personalized ads with your consent and, in Europe, Google's consent form; and Google Play's age signal.
